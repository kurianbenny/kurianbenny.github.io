## A website people can understand

A business website should explain your offer, answer useful questions, and make the next step easy. The design, content structure, and publishing setup should support that job.

## How I can help

- Develop responsive business websites and content pages.
- Structure services, articles, FAQs, and contact journeys.
- Implement titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemaps, and appropriate structured data.
- Review crawlability and the technical foundations that support search and AI-assisted discovery.

## Set a practical scope

We start with your audience, existing website, and the pages that matter most. Search visibility depends on more than technical setup, so the scope focuses on concrete improvements rather than ranking promises.

Read [how to improve your website’s SEO](/blog/improve-your-website-seo/) for a practical starting point.

## Design and content should work together

A clear service page, a useful FAQ, and a contact journey that works on a phone are part of the same website experience. Technical SEO gives that content a more understandable structure for discovery.

## What to share before we begin

Your current website or brief, the audience you want to reach, the services you offer, and the action you want visitors to take. We can then identify the pages and technical changes that deserve attention first.
