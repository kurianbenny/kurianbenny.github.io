## Software aligned with your operational reality

Odoo provides a flexible suite of business applications, but out-of-the-box defaults rarely match every company's exact operational flow. My development background in Python, relational databases, and enterprise architecture helps me customize Odoo to match your business needs rather than forcing your team into awkward workarounds.

## How I can help

- Develop custom Odoo modules and extend core business models (Sales, Invoicing, Inventory, CRM, Purchase).
- Design and automate approval flows, document transitions, and status triggers.
- Build secure REST and XML-RPC API integrations between Odoo and external applications, storefronts, or payment gateways.
- Create custom reports, QWeb templates, and filtered data views for operations and accounting teams.
- Audit and debug existing custom modules, handle version migrations, and optimize database queries.

## Clean modular architecture over fragile hacks

Every customization is built as a modular add-on adhering to Odoo's recommended inheritance and extension patterns. That keeps the core codebase clean, ensures upgradeability across versions, and makes future development maintainable.

## Understand the workflow before modifying the system

Before writing code or changing field behaviors, we map the actual business process: what data enters the system, which team members interact with it, and what operational step follows. Scoping custom logic against real daily workflows prevents over-engineering and keeps the software fast and intuitive.

## What to share before we begin

Your Odoo version and edition (Community or Enterprise), whether you are self-hosting or on Odoo.sh, the modules currently in use, and a description of the workflow or feature you need built or customized.
