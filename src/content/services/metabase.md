## Turn application data into operational clarity

Your application or business database holds valuable insights, but extracting them shouldn't require custom ad-hoc engineering queries every day. I help teams deploy, configure, and model Metabase so founders, operators, and product managers can answer their own questions in seconds.

## How I can help

- Deploy self-hosted Metabase instances on AWS, DigitalOcean, or private VPS infrastructure with Docker and secure reverse proxying.
- Connect and model relational databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MariaDB) and external analytics data sources.
- Build executive KPI dashboards, operational metrics, cohort retention views, and sales funnel visualisations.
- Implement automated report delivery via scheduled email pulses and Slack channels.
- Embed interactive, sandboxed Metabase dashboards into customer portals or SaaS web applications.
- Optimize slow analytical queries with database indexing, read replicas, and aggregated reporting tables.

## Self-serve discovery with proper data boundaries

The best business intelligence setup lets non-technical stakeholders explore trends without risking database performance or exposing sensitive information. We configure role-based permissions, data sandboxing, and curated collections so everyone gets the data they need safely.

## Clear metrics over visual clutter

Dashboards should drive decisions, not display vanity numbers. We begin by defining the core questions your team asks each week: churn rates, active accounts, inventory velocity, or revenue run rates. The dashboards are then structured to make anomalies and trends obvious at a glance.

## What to share before we begin

The databases you intend to connect, where your systems are hosted, the primary business metrics you want to monitor, and whether you plan to self-host or use Metabase Cloud.
