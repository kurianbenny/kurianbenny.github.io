## Software shaped around the workflow

My ERPNext knowledge complements my wider development experience. It gives me a useful starting point for discussing business workflows, integrations, and changes around an ERP system.

## How I can help

- Explore how your processes map to ERPNext.
- Discuss customization and integration requirements.
- Connect ERP workflows with other web applications.
- Assess the development scope for an existing setup.

## Understand the process first

We begin with how the business works today and where the friction is. Any implementation scope is based on those requirements and the existing ERPNext setup.

## Connect the requirements with the system

An ERP change starts with the business process: what information comes in, who works with it, and what should happen next. Discussing the process before a customization helps keep the software aligned with the actual work.

## What to share before we begin

Your ERPNext version, the modules in use, the workflow you want to change, and any systems that need to exchange information. These details help define an appropriate implementation scope.
